About us

A poultry partner for farmers of every scale

AVSL Chicks Ventures Ltd is a poultry-focused company committed to partnering with farmers for better poultry farming. We supply quality poultry products, equipment and practical solutions designed to make poultry farming easier, more reliable and more profitable.

Our core business is day-old broiler and layer chicks, poultry cages, feeds, feeding equipment and other accessories. We serve small-scale, medium-scale and commercial farmers across Kenya, with a long-term vision of expanding into the East African market.

Vision. To become a leading and trusted poultry solutions company in Kenya and East Africa, empowering farmers with quality products, reliable services and innovative solutions.

Mission. To provide quality chicks, feeds, cages, equipment and solutions that help farmers start, operate and grow successful poultry enterprises.

Layer contract farming & services

A structured pathway from day-old chick to active layer

AVSL supplies layer day-old chicks (DOCs) to participating farmers and supports them in rearing and managing the birds up to approximately 2½ months of age. The company then buys back the birds according to agreed terms — giving farmers support during early rearing and a structured market pathway.

1

Day-old chicks

Layer DOCs supplied to participating farmers under the contract program.

2

Pullets

Developed young layer birds for farmers who prefer to start beyond early brooding.

3

Point-of-lay

Birds approaching the stage of commencing egg production.

4

Active layers

Mature, productive birds for farmers seeking established egg-producing stock.

Quantities, pricing, bird quality requirements, timelines and buy-back terms are agreed with participating farmers before commencement.

Broiler contract farming & farmer support

Reducing the cost of commercial broiler production

AVSL offers a broiler contract farming arrangement designed to support farmers in commercial broiler production. Participating farmers receive a 40% support contribution on broiler feed costs, subject to the applicable contract terms and conditions.

Specific quantities, pricing, feed support arrangements, production requirements, timelines and performance expectations are agreed with participating farmers before commencement.
